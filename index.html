    What does this tool check?

    SSL certificate — is it valid, trusted, and not about to expire? HTTPS enforcement — does the plain-HTTP address force visitors onto the secure version? Cookies — do they carry the Secure, HttpOnly and SameSite flags that protect them? Mixed content — does a secure page secretly load files over plain HTTP? And security headers — the instructions (CSP, HSTS and more) that tell the browser how to protect the page. Together they give one honest picture of how secure a website is.
